What happens on submit?
The record is created at Received, registered, passed through extraction review (marked "human entered — no extraction performed"), then validated. Missing PO, missing receipt confirmation, missing required evidence, a duplicate vendor and number, or a declared banking change each create a visible typed exception. Blocking exceptions prevent approval until a human resolves or formally overrides them. No autonomous approval occurs.

Persistence · Export · Import

Keep the week’s work safe.

Every change is saved automatically in this browser. Up to seven daily recovery copies are also kept here. Export a full-session backup before changing computers or browsers.

⚠
Use the same computer and browser throughout the week. Closing the tab or restarting the computer does not normally erase the work. Private/incognito windows, clearing site data, or some privacy extensions can remove it. Download Full session JSON at the end of each day as a separate recovery copy.

What this prototype is not

  • Not authenticated. The role selector simulates a role; it is not a login and enforces nothing outside this browser tab.
  • Not shared. Two people using it see two unrelated sessions.
  • Not connected to accounting, project management, e-mail or a vendor master.
  • Not processing documents. No OCR, no extraction, no confidence scores.
  • Not running an AI model. AVA output is deterministic JavaScript over the records in this browser.
  • Not moving money. No payment is scheduled, released or executed anywhere.
